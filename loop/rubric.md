# Content rubric — gjh-inc.com

Version: 1
Last changed by: human (initial)

Each criterion is scored 0-5. A score of 3 is "acceptable, shipped as-is".
Anything below 3 must produce a concrete `fix` with the exact replacement text.

## 1. Problem-first (weight 1.0)
Does the page open with the buyer's problem, or with GJH's capability?
Pages that open with what GJH does score no higher than 2.

## 2. Falsifiability (weight 1.5)
Every claim must be checkable. Invented metrics, unattributed percentages,
and vague superlatives score 0 for this criterion regardless of how good the
prose reads. This is the criterion that protects us legally and in procurement.

## 3. Differentiation (weight 1.5)
Could a competitor paste this copy onto their own site without changing
anything? If yes, score 1 or below. GJH's thesis — advisory before building,
starting small with a paid assessment of one workflow, senior people doing the
work, and the client owning everything built with no vendor dependency — must be
present and specific.

## 4. Buyer fit (weight 1.0)
Does the language match how a commercial or non-profit leader actually talks
about putting AI to work? Practical framing (where AI is and isn't worth the
effort, real systems, data foundations) scores higher than transformation
promise language.

## 5. Proof density (weight 1.0)
Tenure (founded 2009), the partnerships (Anthropic, Google, AWS, Databricks,
Snowflake), named platforms and named
deliverables per engagement. Generic trust language with nothing behind it
scores 1.

## 6. Machine legibility (weight 1.0)
Is the substance in server-rendered text, with schema markup, headings that
state claims rather than labels? Buyers increasingly arrive via an agent that
reads the page. A page whose value proposition exists only in an image or a
JS-hydrated component scores 0 here.

## 7. Voice discipline (weight 0.5)
No banned hype vocabulary. Short sentences. No em-dash-free run-ons.
This is the lowest weight on purpose — style is real but it is not the moat.
